## Meal photos and calorie estimates

A meal photo can provide an initial food and nutrition estimate. Check portions, ingredients and cooking oil before saving. Food calories are commonly reported in kcal, also called kilocalories.

| Common terms | Meaning in Lenjo |
| --- | --- |
| photo calorie estimation / food photo calorie estimate / picture-based calorie estimate | Estimate meal energy from an image; the image cannot establish every ingredient or actual weight. |
| food recognition / meal recognition / AI food identification | Identify visible food items, then correct items the model has mistaken. |
| calorie estimate / kcal estimate / kilocalorie estimate | Translate estimated food portions into nutrition references, then confirm the record. |
| photo food logging / photo meal logging / meal photo analysis | Start a meal record with a camera or gallery photo, review the analysis and save it. |

### How many calories are in this meal?

Analyze a photo, then check food types, portions, sauces, cooking oil and anything outside the picture before confirming.

### Can I estimate food calories from a gallery photo?

Select a meal image for analysis. Lighting and occlusion affect the estimate, so review before saving.

### How do I log my portion of a shared meal?

Meal scans support shared-table portions and separate entries. Adjust to the portion you ate instead of recording the entire table.

## Food diaries and macro tracking

Nutrition tracking starts with reviewable records. Enter food manually or analyze a photo, confirm calories and macros, then discuss the records with the coach.

| Common terms | Meaning in Lenjo |
| --- | --- |
| diet tracking / food diary / food journal | Save foods eaten by meal; manual entry is available without photo analysis. |
| calorie tracking / calorie logging / food calorie log | Record energy intake separately from workout energy expenditure. |
| protein tracking / protein intake log / dietary protein tracking | Record estimated or manually entered protein intake as context for nutrition and training discussion. |
| macro tracking / macronutrient logging / carbs and fat tracking | Review protein, carbohydrates and fat separately; values depend on food and portion estimates. |

### Can I log food without taking a photo?

Yes. Enter food and nutrition manually; photo analysis is optional.

### Can I track protein, carbs and fat?

Yes. Track all three macros; photo-derived values are estimates to review.

### Are food calories and workout calories the same record?

They describe intake and expenditure respectively. Lenjo keeps meal and workout context; consider data sources and uncertainty when discussing them.

## AI fitness coaching

Lenjo connects coaching conversation to workout execution: explain constraints, review a plan, log actual performance and use saved results to discuss the next session.

| Common terms | Meaning in Lenjo |
| --- | --- |
| AI fitness / AI fitness app / smart fitness assistant | Use training goals and actual records as context for fitness references and planning. |
| AI coach / AI fitness coach / AI workout coach | Ask the coach about exercises, sets, reps, load and rest. |
| AI personal trainer / virtual fitness coach / fitness chatbot | These describe software coaching; Lenjo does not provide in-person observation or medical diagnosis. |
| personalized workout advice / history-based workout coaching / workout review conversation | Use saved workouts and permitted recovery context to discuss the reasoning behind next-session suggestions. |

### Can an AI fitness coach use my previous workouts?

The coach can use saved workouts and permitted context. Unsaved, unprovided or missing data cannot be treated as reliable history.

### Can AI coaching turn into an executable workout?

Review and edit a structured plan, start the workout and log what you actually complete.

### Can I track workouts without chatting with AI?

Yes. iPhone workout execution and logging work independently. AI chat needs internet, consent and an available allowance.

| Common terms | Meaning in Lenjo |
| --- | --- |
| between-set recovery / rest between sets / next-set readiness guidance | Use heart rate, elapsed rest and training context as references for the next set. |

| recovery context logging / fatigue-based workout adjustment / deload discussion | Describe fatigue and recent performance to discuss conservative changes and their reasoning. |

### How long should I rest between sets?

Review planned rest and available Watch guidance alongside your experience. Do not let a cue override discomfort.

### How can I adjust training after poor sleep?

Tell the coach about sleep and how you feel, then discuss changes using saved workouts and permitted context.

### Can I train without heart-rate or HRV data?

Yes. Log workouts and perceived effort; missing health data should not be treated as a normal measurement.

## Local records, health permissions and coaching memory

Local-first describes record storage. AI coaching and photo analysis need internet and consent; coaching memory can be inspected, edited or deleted.

| Common terms | Meaning in Lenjo |
| --- | --- |
| local workout logging / local-first fitness app / offline workout logging | Core records stay primarily on device; workout execution and logging remain available without AI. |
| Apple Health training context / HealthKit fitness context / health data permissions | Apple Health permissions are optional; coaching can use only permitted available information. |
| AI coaching memory / personalized coaching context / training preference memory | Keep goals, equipment and stable preferences as context for future discussions. |
| edit coaching memory / delete coaching memory / inspect coach context | Inspect and correct remembered information with user-controlled editing and deletion. |

### Can I log workouts without internet?

Local workout execution and logging remain available; AI chat and photo analysis need internet.

### What if the AI coach remembers my equipment or goal incorrectly?

Inspect coaching memory, edit or delete incorrect details and state your current constraints.

### Does all health data stay on my phone?

Core records are primarily on device. With AI consent, relevant content is processed through the gateway and third-party model providers.
