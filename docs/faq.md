# Lenjo FAQ / 练境常见问题

For detailed terms and practical questions, read the [English feature guide](feature-guide.md) or [中文功能指南](feature-guide.zh-CN.md).

## 可以拍照识别热量和卡路里吗？ / Can a meal photo estimate calories?

可以。餐食照片分析估算食物、热量（kcal）和蛋白质、碳水、脂肪。检查份量、酱汁和烹调用油，修改后再确认保存。多人合餐时需要按实际吃的部分调整。

Yes. Meal photo analysis estimates food items, calories (kcal), protein, carbohydrates and fat. Check portions, sauces and cooking oil, then edit and confirm. For shared meals, adjust to what you ate.

## AI 饮食可以和健身管理一起用吗？ / Can AI discuss nutrition and workouts together?

可以结合已保存的饮食、体重目标、实际训练与恢复信息，向教练询问营养和下一次训练。建议取决于数据完整程度，需要审核。

Discuss saved meals, weight goals, actual workouts and recovery context with the coach. Review suggestions in light of the completeness of the data.

## What is Lenjo? / 练境是什么？

Lenjo (练境) is an iPhone AI fitness coach and workout tracker with optional Apple Watch support. It connects plans, completed sets, recovery context and nutrition records.

练境是 iPhone 上的 AI 健身教练与训练记录 App，可选配 Apple Watch，把计划、实际完成组、恢复和饮食记录接起来。

## Do I need an Apple Watch? / 必须有手表吗？

No. iPhone can independently run and log workouts. Apple Watch execution is optional and requires Pro, a paired Watch and the Watch app. Heart-rate features depend on available data and permissions.

不需要。iPhone 可独立执行并保存训练；手表为可选设备，腕上训练需 Pro、已配对的 Watch 和练境 Watch App，心率功能还需要权限及可用数据。

## Is Lenjo free? / 免费版能做什么？

iPhone workout execution and logging are free, with an AI trial allowance. Pro adds Watch execution and recovery guidance, higher AI allowances and deeper reports. Check the app for current prices and limits.

iPhone 训练执行与记录免费，并有 AI 体验额度。Pro 提供腕上执行与恢复参考、更高 AI 额度及深度报告；价格和限制以 App 内为准。

## Does a plan become an actual workout? / 计划能直接执行吗？

Yes. Review and edit the plan before starting, then log the sets you actually complete and save the workout. The coach can use saved records when you discuss the next session.

可以。先审核并修改计划，再逐组记录实际完成情况并保存，之后向教练讨论下一次训练。

## Can I log cardio and meals? / 支持有氧与饮食吗？

Yes. Cardio sessions can be timed, with relevant speed, incline and distance notes. Meals can be entered manually or estimated from photos, then reviewed and confirmed before saving.

支持。有氧按分钟记录，并填写相关速度、坡度与距离；饮食可以手动记录，也可以拍照估算后检查、修改并确认保存。

## Is all my data uploaded? / 数据会全部上传吗？

Core records are primarily local. AI features need a network connection and process relevant information with third-party model providers after explanation and consent. You can decline AI and keep using local logging. See the privacy policy for details.

核心记录主要保存在本机。AI 功能需要联网，并在说明与同意后把相关内容交给第三方模型服务处理；拒绝 AI 后仍可用本地记录。具体以隐私政策为准。

## Can I inspect what the coach remembers? / 能查看教练记住的内容吗？

Yes. Long-term memories can be viewed, edited or deleted in the app. They include stable context such as goals, equipment and preferences.

可以。App 内的长期记忆可查看、编辑或删除，包括目标、器械和偏好等长期信息。

## Does it diagnose injuries or guarantee accurate nutrition? / 能诊断伤病或保证营养估算准确吗？

No. Lenjo provides fitness references, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Photo estimates and recovery guidance require context and user review.

练境提供健身参考，不诊断或治疗伤病。照片估算和恢复建议需要结合具体情况，由用户检查确认。
