# Getting started with Lenjo / 练境上手步骤

## English

1. Install [Lenjo from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784486285) on an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
2. Enter your training goals, experience, available equipment and preferences. Review the AI processing explanation if you want to use coaching or photo analysis; Apple Health access is optional.
3. Ask the coach for a session, for example: “Plan a 45-minute strength workout with the equipment I have.” Review the proposed exercises and targets before starting. You can also use a free workout without asking AI for a plan.
4. Record the actual weight, reps and RPE for each completed set. Save the workout when finished. Use timed cardio logging when relevant.
5. For Apple Watch execution, use Lenjo Pro with a paired Watch and the Lenjo Watch app installed. Grant relevant health permissions if you want heart-rate and recovery features.
6. Review your saved workout and ask the coach about the next session. For meals, review and edit photo-analysis estimates before confirming them.

## 中文

1. 在 iOS 17 及以上的 iPhone 上从 [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784486285) 安装练境。
2. 填写训练目标、经验、可用器械和偏好。使用 AI 教练或照片分析时先阅读处理说明；Apple 健康权限按需授权。
3. 请求训练计划，例如：“按我现有器械安排一场 45 分钟力量训练。”先审核动作和目标，再开始；也可以直接开始自由训练。
4. 逐组记录实际重量、次数、RPE 与完成状态，结束后保存。有氧训练按分钟记录。
5. 如需腕上执行，使用 Lenjo Pro，并在已配对的 Apple Watch 上安装练境。心率与恢复功能需要相应健康权限及可用数据。
6. 用保存的训练记录向教练讨论下一次调整；拍照记饮食时先检查并修改估算结果，再确认保存。
