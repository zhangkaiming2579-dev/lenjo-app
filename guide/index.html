Meal photos and calorie estimates

A meal photo can provide an initial food and nutrition estimate. Check portions, ingredients and cooking oil before saving. Food calories are commonly reported in kcal, also called kilocalories.

Terms and definitions

photo calorie estimation / food photo calorie estimate / picture-based calorie estimate
Estimate meal energy from an image; the image cannot establish every ingredient or actual weight.
food recognition / meal recognition / AI food identification
Identify visible food items, then correct items the model has mistaken.
calorie estimate / kcal estimate / kilocalorie estimate
Translate estimated food portions into nutrition references, then confirm the record.
photo food logging / photo meal logging / meal photo analysis
Start a meal record with a camera or gallery photo, review the analysis and save it.

Practical questions

How many calories are in this meal?

Analyze a photo, then check food types, portions, sauces, cooking oil and anything outside the picture before confirming.

Can I estimate food calories from a gallery photo?

Select a meal image for analysis. Lighting and occlusion affect the estimate, so review before saving.

How do I log my portion of a shared meal?

Meal scans support shared-table portions and separate entries. Adjust to the portion you ate instead of recording the entire table.

Food diaries and macro tracking

Nutrition tracking starts with reviewable records. Enter food manually or analyze a photo, confirm calories and macros, then discuss the records with the coach.

Terms and definitions

diet tracking / food diary / food journal
Save foods eaten by meal; manual entry is available without photo analysis.
calorie tracking / calorie logging / food calorie log
Record energy intake separately from workout energy expenditure.
protein tracking / protein intake log / dietary protein tracking
Record estimated or manually entered protein intake as context for nutrition and training discussion.
macro tracking / macronutrient logging / carbs and fat tracking
Review protein, carbohydrates and fat separately; values depend on food and portion estimates.

Practical questions

Can I log food without taking a photo?

Yes. Enter food and nutrition manually; photo analysis is optional.

Can I track protein, carbs and fat?

Yes. Track all three macros; photo-derived values are estimates to review.

Are food calories and workout calories the same record?

They describe intake and expenditure respectively. Lenjo keeps meal and workout context; consider data sources and uncertainty when discussing them.

AI nutrition and diet discussion

AI nutrition discussion uses the information you provide and save. Explain weight goals, recent meals and training plans to discuss protein intake and meals around workouts.

Terms and definitions

AI nutrition assistant / AI diet assistant / AI nutrition coaching
Discuss saved meals with the coach; review suggestions before acting.
diet analysis / nutrition analysis / meal nutrition review
Discuss confirmed food and nutrient records; add context when information is incomplete.
fat-loss food diary / calorie deficit discussion / weight-goal nutrition
Discuss intake and activity in light of weight goals; a single meal estimate is not an exact daily energy balance.
muscle-gain nutrition discussion / pre-workout nutrition / post-workout nutrition
Discuss meals around strength training using recent records, without a universal meal-plan promise.

Practical questions

Can Lenjo help with AI diet tracking?

It supports manual meals, photo estimates and coach discussion based on records. Confirm records before asking about changes.

Did I eat enough protein today?

Save today's meals and tell the coach your weight goal and training context. Advice depends on record completeness.

Can I discuss nutrition and training for fat loss or muscle gain together?

Discuss workout records, meals and weight goals together and review actual changes over time. Outcomes are not guaranteed.

AI fitness coaching

Lenjo connects coaching conversation to workout execution: explain constraints, review a plan, log actual performance and use saved results to discuss the next session.

Terms and definitions

AI fitness / AI fitness app / smart fitness assistant
Use training goals and actual records as context for fitness references and planning.
AI coach / AI fitness coach / AI workout coach
Ask the coach about exercises, sets, reps, load and rest.
AI personal trainer / virtual fitness coach / fitness chatbot
These describe software coaching; Lenjo does not provide in-person observation or medical diagnosis.
personalized workout advice / history-based workout coaching / workout review conversation
Use saved workouts and permitted recovery context to discuss the reasoning behind next-session suggestions.

Practical questions

Can an AI fitness coach use my previous workouts?

The coach can use saved workouts and permitted context. Unsaved, unprovided or missing data cannot be treated as reliable history.

Can AI coaching turn into an executable workout?

Review and edit a structured plan, start the workout and log what you actually complete.

Can I track workouts without chatting with AI?

Yes. iPhone workout execution and logging work independently. AI chat needs internet, consent and an available allowance.

Workout plans and scheduling

Tell the coach your equipment, experience, weekly availability and session duration. Check exercises, sets, reps and load before starting a plan.

Terms and definitions

workout plan / training plan / AI workout plan
Request a structured plan that can be reviewed and linked to set-by-set logging.
strength training plan / muscle-building workout plan / fat-loss workout planning
Discuss strength training for your goals and constraints; a goal is not an outcome guarantee.
weekly workout schedule / workout split / split routine planning
Provide training days and your split as context for future planning discussions.
today's workout / next workout planning / workout plan adjustment
Discuss a specific session using saved records and today's constraints, then revise suggestions as needed.

Practical questions

What should I train today?

Explain your time, equipment, recent workouts and recovery, request a session and review it before starting.

How can I plan workouts for three days a week?

Share available days, session length and goals. Ask for the reasoning and revise using actual completed workouts.

Can I edit exercises or weights in a plan?

Review and change exercises, sets, reps and weights before starting, then log actual performance.

strength training tracker / muscle-building workout log / resistance training log

training volume / workout volume tracking / training load trends
Review volume-related trends in context of exercises and time periods.

Can I review a month of training volume?

Is the 1RM value a measured max?

It is an estimate. Interpret it in light of the exercise, record quality and your condition.

Terms and definitions

beginner workout plan / beginner fitness planning / beginner strength training

dumbbell workout planning / bodyweight workout planning / no-equipment workout advice

Can I plan a workout with only half an hour?

Apple Watch workout execution

Watch execution is an optional Pro feature. Pair a Watch, install the Lenjo Watch app and allow required health data. iPhone logging works independently.

Terms and definitions

Apple Watch fitness app / Apple Watch strength training / Watch workout logging
Execute a plan on a paired Watch and sync completed results back to iPhone.
Watch workout plan / wrist workout execution / Watch set logging
View the plan and log actual reps, load and completion from your wrist.
live workout heart rate / per-set heart rate / workout heart-rate timeline
Relate heart-rate samples to set timings when permissions and usable samples are available.
Watch rest between sets / recovery haptic cue / wrist recovery guidance
Review recovery between sets and receive haptic cues when conditions support them; signals may be incomplete.

Practical questions

Can Apple Watch log each strength-training set?

Pro supports wrist execution and logging with the paired Watch app. Edit actual reps when needed.

Does the Watch automatically count my reps?

No. Target reps are prefilled and you adjust them to actual performance.

Can I use Lenjo without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Execute, log sets and save sessions on iPhone alone.

Recovery context and rest between sets

Recovery guidance needs data and your reported experience. Permitted, available health data can inform training discussion but cannot establish movement quality or medical status.

Terms and definitions

between-set recovery / rest between sets / next-set readiness guidance
Use heart rate, elapsed rest and training context as references for the next set.
heart-rate recovery / heart-rate drop / recovery readiness reference
Provide fitness pacing references from available signals, without clinical diagnostic meaning.
sleep and training context / HRV training context / resting heart rate and training
Permitted available health information can inform coaching alongside history and reported experience.
recovery context logging / fatigue-based workout adjustment / deload discussion
Describe fatigue and recent performance to discuss conservative changes and their reasoning.

Practical questions

How long should I rest between sets?

Review planned rest and available Watch guidance alongside your experience. Do not let a cue override discomfort.

How can I adjust training after poor sleep?

Tell the coach about sleep and how you feel, then discuss changes using saved workouts and permitted context.

Can I train without heart-rate or HRV data?

Yes. Log workouts and perceived effort; missing health data should not be treated as a normal measurement.

Local records, health permissions and coaching memory

Local-first describes record storage. AI coaching and photo analysis need internet and consent; coaching memory can be inspected, edited or deleted.

Terms and definitions

local workout logging / local-first fitness app / offline workout logging
Core records stay primarily on device; workout execution and logging remain available without AI.
Apple Health training context / HealthKit fitness context / health data permissions
Apple Health permissions are optional; coaching can use only permitted available information.
AI coaching memory / personalized coaching context / training preference memory
Keep goals, equipment and stable preferences as context for future discussions.
edit coaching memory / delete coaching memory / inspect coach context
Inspect and correct remembered information with user-controlled editing and deletion.

Practical questions

Can I log workouts without internet?

Local workout execution and logging remain available; AI chat and photo analysis need internet.

What if the AI coach remembers my equipment or goal incorrectly?

Inspect coaching memory, edit or delete incorrect details and state your current constraints.

Does all health data stay on my phone?

Core records are primarily on device. With AI consent, relevant content is processed through the gateway and third-party model providers.
