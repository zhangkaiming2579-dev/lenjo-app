02 / FEATURES

From a suggested plan to completed sets.

01

AI workout planning

Describe your goals, equipment, experience and available time. The coach can use saved workout history and permitted recovery context to suggest exercises, sets, reps, load and rest. Review the plan before starting.

02

Set-by-set workout tracking

Start a planned session or a free workout. Record actual weights, reps, RPE and completed sets on iPhone, then save and review them before discussing the next session.

03

Apple Watch recovery guidance

With Pro, send a plan to a paired Watch and log sets from your wrist. Available health data can support live heart-rate recovery guidance and a post-workout heart-rate-by-set timeline.

04

Meal photo and nutrition logging

Enter food manually or analyze a meal photo. Review estimated items, calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat, edit what needs correcting, then confirm before saving.

05

Timed cardio sessions

Record cardio by time, with speed, incline and distance notes where relevant. Pro users can record available heart-rate, calorie and distance data with Apple Watch.

06

Progress and coaching memory

Review body-weight, activity, recovery and training trends. Inspect, edit or delete long-term coaching memories, including your goals, equipment and preferences.

04 / IN THE APP

See the workout workflow.

These public product screenshots contain example training data. The interface may vary by version and language. Recovery guidance is a fitness reference, not a medical measurement.

Free logging. Optional Pro.

iPhone workout execution and logging are free, with an AI trial allowance. Pro adds Watch workout execution and recovery guidance, higher AI coach and photo-analysis allowances, and deeper reports. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available; check the app for current prices and limits.

Local records. AI by consent.

Core records are primarily stored on your device. Apple sign-in and Apple Health permissions are optional. AI coaching and photo analysis require internet access and process relevant information through the Lenjo gateway and third-party model providers after explanation and consent. You can decline AI and keep using local logging.

05 / FAQ

Questions about Lenjo.

What is Lenjo?

Lenjo (练境) is a local-first AI fitness coach and workout tracker for iPhone. It connects structured workout plans, actual training records, recovery context and nutrition logging.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. iPhone can independently execute and log workouts. Watch training is optional and requires Pro, a paired Watch and the Lenjo Watch app. Heart-rate features require relevant permissions and usable data.

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. If you decline AI processing, local workout execution and logging remain available. AI coaching and photo analysis require an internet connection and consent.

Can the plan be executed in the app?

Yes. Review and edit the suggested plan, start the session and record the sets you actually complete. Save the results before discussing your next workout.

Can I record meals from photos?

Yes. Meal photos can be analyzed for estimated food items and nutrition. Review, correct and confirm the result before saving; a photo cannot reliably determine every ingredient or portion.
