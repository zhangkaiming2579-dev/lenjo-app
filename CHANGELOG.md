# Public release notes

## 1.0.18 — App Store version checked 2026-10-11

- Timed cardio logging, including speed, incline and distance notes; Apple Watch recording for Pro users when supported data is available.
- Past workouts can be imported from a pasted text log, including cardio time and distance.
- Exercise library expanded to 160 exercises with 40 additional illustrations.
- Upcoming plans appear in the calendar and can be opened to start early.
- Watch workouts show calories in history and share posters; photo check-in posters include a monthly training calendar and photo timestamp.
- Meal scans support shared-table portions and separate entries within a meal.
- Photo-scan allowance is checked before the camera opens.

This summary is based on the public App Store release notes. The check date is not the release date. See the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6784486285) for current availability.
